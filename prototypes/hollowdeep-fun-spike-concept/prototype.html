HOLLOWDEEP — fun spike

Build your architecture, then defend it turn-by-turn. The walls you choose are the battlefield. (Greybox prototype — everything is placeholder.)

Build — stone:

Click/drag on the map to build. Demolish refunds cost. Raiders come from the LEFT edge (bedrock — unbuildable). They want the gold hoard, and they will break through your weakest architecture to get it.

Battle —

Click one of your defenders.

      How to play: Build mode — pick a tool, paint walls/doors, position your 3 crossbow-colonists (squad tool), then sound the alarm. Battle — each colonist has 2 actions: move (up to 4 cells), shoot (range 7, needs line of sight — walls and CLOSED doors block it, for you and for them), or toggle an adjacent door. Raiders smash what's in their way; higher wall tiers take longer to smash. Loot stolen and walls lost stay lost. Each raid is bigger than the last.
